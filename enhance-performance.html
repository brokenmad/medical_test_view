Frequently
Asked Questions

Answered by Dr. Lonny Miller, MD — a clinician who has helped thousands of men reclaim their energy, confidence, and vitality through personalized, evidence-based care.

What are peptides, and how do they work?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids found naturally in the body. In a clinical setting, compounded peptide products may be used when a provider determines they may be appropriate for an individual’s care plan.

Are peptides safe?

Peptides are prescribed only when a licensed provider determines they may be appropriate for your custom treatment plan.

How soon will I see results?

Experiences vary from person to person. Your provider can discuss what to expect based on your health needs and the specifics of your treatment plan.

Are these injections or pills?

Most peptides are administered through subcutaneous injections, but some are available in oral or nasal spray form. Our providers will walk you through the best method for your treatment.

Can I combine peptides with other therapies like TRT or GLP-1s?

Absolutely. In fact, peptides often enhance the results of other therapies. We’ll help you build a personalized treatment plan that’s safe and effective.

Is there a one-size-fits-all peptide program?

Nope. Peptide protocols differ from person to person. Your clinician will recommend an approach based on your individual evaluation and overall health goals.
